Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

PostingClips ("PostingClips", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the PostingClips application and related website.

1. Information We Access

PostingClips may access information necessary to provide its video publishing functionality when you authorize the application through a supported platform.

This may include:

  • Basic account information provided by the platform.
  • Authorization tokens required to perform permitted actions.
  • Information necessary to publish videos on your behalf.
  • Video files, captions, titles and descriptions that you choose to process or publish.

2. Social Media Account Access

PostingClips uses official APIs and authorization systems provided by supported social media platforms.

PostingClips does not request or require your social media password. Authentication is handled by the applicable platform.

You can revoke the application's access through the account settings provided by the applicable social media platform.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by PostingClips may be used to:

  • Authenticate your account.
  • Prepare video content.
  • Publish videos that you instruct the application to publish.
  • Maintain authorization sessions.
  • Prevent duplicate publishing when supported by the application.
  • Provide and maintain the application's functionality.

4. Video Content

Videos and related content processed by PostingClips are used to perform the actions requested by the user.

The application does not claim ownership of your video content. You remain responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights and permissions to use and publish any content processed through the application.

5. Information Sharing

PostingClips does not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to a social media platform when you specifically request that PostingClips publish content to that platform. The information transmitted is limited to what is necessary for the requested publishing operation and what the applicable platform API requires.

6. Third-Party Services

PostingClips may interact with third-party services including YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and their respective APIs. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. Your use of those services is also subject to their respective policies.

7. Data Retention

PostingClips retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality, maintain authorization, or comply with applicable obligations.

Local files created by the application, including processed videos and authorization files, may remain on the user's computer until the user deletes them.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by PostingClips. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

PostingClips is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to provide consent to online services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Your Choices

You may stop using PostingClips at any time. You may also revoke the application's authorization through the relevant social media platform.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or PostingClips, please contact the application owner.
