About PostingClips

PostingClips is designed to simplify the process of preparing and publishing short-form video content.

🎬

Prepare Videos

Videos can be divided into shorter segments and formatted for vertical short-form platforms.

✨

Customize Content

Creators can add captions, subtitles, titles, descriptions and other visual elements to their videos.

📱

Publish

PostingClips can use supported platform APIs to publish content to accounts authorized by the creator.

Supported Platforms

YouTube Video publishing
Instagram Reels publishing
TikTok Video publishing

PostingClips only accesses social media accounts when the account owner explicitly authorizes the application.

Account Authorization

PostingClips does not ask for or store your social media password. Authentication is handled through the applicable platform's authorization system.

You may revoke PostingClips' access through the settings provided by the applicable social media platform.

Contact

For questions about PostingClips, privacy, or the use of this application, please contact the application owner.
